# 16 - Case Studies Gallery (with charts)

This page collects **all 24 examples** from `usage_examples/` with the charts
generated by beamfeapy. Images are produced by running the real examples
(`python scripts/_gen_example_images.py`, requires `beamfeapy[plot]`); examples
01, 10, 16, 17, 18, 20, 21 have only textual output and produce no charts.

> European convention in moment diagrams: **negative moment at the extrados**
> (positive/hogging at the intrados).

---

### 01 — Cantilever with tip load
Simplest example: fixed cantilever with vertical force at the free end.
Verifies `δ = PL³/3EI` (error ~1e-16). *(text output only)*
